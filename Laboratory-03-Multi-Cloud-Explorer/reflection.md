## Mission Reflection

**1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most, and why?**
The cloud platform that impressed me the most was **AWS** because it offers different services that can be used for storage and other cloud needs. I found it useful because it provides several options that can support different types of users and businesses.

**2. What similarities did you notice between AWS, Azure, and GCP?**
I noticed that **AWS, Azure, and GCP** have many similarities because they all provide cloud storage, computing services, and tools for managing data and applications. Although their interfaces and features may be different, they can all be used to support business and technology needs.

**3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?**
Business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform because a company needs to consider its **budget, storage needs, security, performance, and required services**. There is no single platform that is best for every business, so the choice should depend on what the company actually needs.

As a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that the job is not only about knowing how cloud services work. I also need to understand the client’s problems and requirements so I can recommend a cloud solution that is practical and suitable for their needs.
